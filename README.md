# Online Retail Business Intelligence & Sales Analytics

## Project Overview
This project analyses an online retail dataset using Microsoft Power BI to identify sales trends, customer behaviour, and product performance.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX

## Dataset
UCI Online Retail Dataset.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales Overview
- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Sales by Country
- Monthly Sales Trends
- Monthly Return / Cancellation Quantity

### 2. Customer Analysis
- Customer Segmentation
- Repeat Customer Analysis
- Repeat Customer Rate
- Customer Spending Behaviour

### 3. Product Analysis
- Top 10 Products by Revenue
- Top 10 Products by Quantity Sold
- Product Sales vs Quantity Analysis

## Key Business Insights
- The United Kingdom generated the highest sales.
- November 2011 recorded the highest monthly sales.
- Regency Cake Stand 3 Tier was the highest revenue-generating physical product.
- World War 2 Gliders Assorted Designs had the highest quantity sold.
- Low Value customers represented the largest customer segment.
